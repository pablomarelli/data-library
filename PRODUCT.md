# data-library

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected React, TypeScript, and Bklit charts. Portable static build first. Intended domain: data.pablomarelli.dev.

## Product Purpose

A public collection of curated economic analysis, starting with Argentina. Store reusable economic datasets independently of charts. The first analysis reconstructs the Salario Digno Porcel Jr chart.

## Users

The owner curates analyses. Public visitors explore charts and inspect or download their source data. No accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual Spanish and English interface.
- Both readable light charts with subtle motion and expressive dark charts, switchable for comparison.
- Data catalog with downloads, provenance, coverage, units, and methodology.
- Historical reference rates from 1989 onward, where available; other rate histories retain their actual coverage.
- Independent Porcel reconstruction, not a claim of exact replication.
- Do not invent historical observations or replace missing rates with zero.
- Static hosting; deployment provider remains undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

Research established a reproducible San Luis / GBA / national CPI splice. ArgentinaDatos offers daily financial rates from 2013; OECD/FRED has monthly historical exchange rates. Source methodology and actual downloaded coverage must be recorded.

## Product Principles

- Economic calculations are separate from chart presentation.
- Preserve source data and precision.
- State uncertainty and coverage explicitly.
- Charts share datasets instead of owning private copies.
